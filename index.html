Multimodal Emotion Recognition via Causal-Diffusion BridgeResearch A NOTEARS causal graph re-weights the text, audio and video channels, a β-VAE compresses them, and a stop-gradiented 1D DDPM prior structures the latent space. Counterfactual sampling over that graph makes attribution explicit, so the model reports which modality drove each prediction — an 18% relative improvement on CMU-MOSEI, and the only system with non-trivial performance on the minority emotions where every baseline fails. Google Doc ↗ CoT Faithfulness Under Necessity vs. PropensityProject Misleading authority cues injected into 1,700 problems per model across Qwen3-4B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-8B and Gemma-3-4B, with every captured trace scored — by LLM judge and by hand — for whether the reasoning admitted the cue.
