Multimodal Emotion Recognition via Causal-Diffusion Bridge

2025 · Preprint

A causal graph re-weights text, audio and video; counterfactual sampling makes attribution explicit, so the model reports which modality drove each prediction.

paper ↗write-up →CMU-MOSEI
A NOTEARS causal graph re-weights the text, audio and video channels, a β-VAE compresses them, and a stop-gradiented 1D DDPM prior structures the latent space. Counterfactual sampling over that graph makes attribution explicit — an 18% relative improvement on CMU-MOSEI, and the only system with non-trivial performance on the minority emotions where every baseline fails.

RectifiedHR: High-Resolution Diffusion via Energy Profiling

2025 · Preprint

Profiles latent energy dynamics during sampling and schedules classifier-free guidance from that signal. Training-free high-resolution generation.

paper ↗write-up →SDXL
No fine-tuning and no extra parameters — just a better-behaved guidance schedule derived from the energy of the latent as the model samples.

Local Prompt Adaptation for Style-Consistent Generation

2025 · Preprint

paper ↗write-up →Training-free

CoT Faithfulness Under Necessity vs. Propensity

2026 · BlueDot capstone

1,700 cue-injected problems per model across three families, every trace scored by judge and by hand. In the worst case a model disclosed the cue once in 489 captured traces.

write-up →lesswrong ↗Qwen · DeepSeek · Gemma
Capture is near-zero (0.0–2.2%) where reasoning is computationally necessary and reaches 41% where it is optional. The necessity arm protects monitoring by prevention, not by disclosure.

NEPSE Data Pipeline

2026 · Data engineering
