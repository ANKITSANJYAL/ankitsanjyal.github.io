Three directions

Same content, same two typefaces, three different amounts of atmosphere. All static HTML, no build step, GitHub Pages ready.

1  ·  Graphite

his structure, your palette

His full fixed-frame system — corner clock, mood toggle, persistent nav, content region that swaps in place — rendered strictly in black, white and grey. Square corners kept. Animated greyscale mesh behind glass cards. Your search box survives as a / overlay.

2  ·  Ledger

your restraint, his voice

One scrolling document with a sticky index rail. No animation at all, no glass, no motion — just paper grain, EB Garamond for the prose and JetBrains Mono for the data. Everything on one page, everything indexable, depth via inline expansion.

static · monochrome · one document · scrollspy · search preserved

3  ·  Subconscious

closest to his

The full homage: animated mesh gradient, falling rain, three moods, rounded glass cards, cream-paper modals, and your portrait ordered-dithered on a canvas. This is the one that breaks your no-accent rule — deliberately, so you can see what it costs and what it buys.

animated · tinted · rounded · dithered portrait · modals

Every concept ships real HTML in the document — nothing is injected by JS — so all three keep the crawlability his site gave up. Concept 3 needs to be served over HTTP for the canvas dither (a local server, not file://), otherwise it falls back to a greyscale photo.
