CoT Faithfulness Under Necessity vs. Propensity
AI Safety LessWrong

Misleading "authority" cues injected into 1,700 problems per model across Qwen3-4B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-8B, and Gemma-3-4B, with every captured trace scored — by LLM judge and by hand — for whether the reasoning admitted the cue. Capture is near-zero (0.0–2.2%) where reasoning is computationally necessary and reaches 41% where it is optional. Five-week BlueDot Impact project.

Diffusion Detective: Interpretability Tooling for a Generative Model
Interpretability

Surfaces what a generative model attends to at each denoising step, steers it by injecting a concept vector mid-generation (94% steering success rate), and writes a plain-English explanation of what changed. Extract, intervene, explain — the core primitives of activation steering and patchscopes.

Affect-Diff: Causal-Diffusion Bridge for Interpretable Emotion Recognition
Causal Interpretability

A NOTEARS causal graph re-weights modalities, a β-VAE compresses them, and a stop-gradiented 1D DDPM prior structures the latent space; counterfactual sampling then makes attribution explicit. 18% relative improvement on CMU-MOSEI, with non-trivial performance on minority emotions where all baselines fail.
